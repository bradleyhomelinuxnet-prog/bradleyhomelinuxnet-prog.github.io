Video tools

Claude skills · finished film from footage you own

rogue-walker-studio NEW

Seven Claude skills that turn a folder of clips and a script into a captioned, voiced, loopable reel with frame-exact timing, without spending a generation credit: the rogue-ai director, the palindromic walker-reel engine, a clip captioner, house looks, a FilmCraft MCP bridge, an ArtCraft bridge, and a credit-budget planner. Its example, The Deposition, is 19 clips and 276 seconds (138 × 2).
